A NEW APPROACH TO BATTERY STORAGE

Travel smarter. Carry safer.

Introducing AEROSAFE: a proposed compact protective sleeve designed for MagSafe portable chargers, with travel convenience and battery safety in mind.

DISCOVER AEROSAFE
THE IDEA

Small design. Important purpose.

Portable chargers have become everyday travel essentials. However, lithium-ion batteries can pose fire risks if damaged or improperly handled. AEROSAFE is being developed to explore a more thoughtful way to carry portable batteries.

THE DESIGN

Designed around convenience.

01. MagSafe Fit

A proposed sleeve designed specifically for compatible MagSafe portable chargers.

02. Compact Design

A slim, lightweight concept intended to fit easily into everyday travel bags.

03. Safety-Focused

Material selection and independent testing are planned to evaluate battery-related fire-risk mitigation.

OUR MISSION

Rethinking the way we carry power.

Our goal is to combine thoughtful design, travel convenience, and a stronger focus on portable battery safety. AEROSAFE is currently a developing product concept.
