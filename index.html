The idea

Small design. Important purpose.

Portable chargers have become everyday travel essentials. However, lithium-ion batteries can pose fire risks if damaged or improperly handled. AEROSAFE explores a more thoughtful way to carry portable batteries, in your bag, on the road and in the air.

Why it matters in the air

A small spark shouldn't become a cabin fire.

When a damaged lithium-ion battery overheats at 35,000 feet, the danger isn't just the charger. It's the bag, the seat and the cabin around it. AEROSAFE is designed to keep an overheating charger inside its sleeve, helping contain the heat so a single battery problem is less likely to spread into a larger fire while the crew responds.
